Why cats love a sunny window

A warm patch of sunlight is more than a cozy place to nap. Windows offer cats changing sights, sounds, and smells that keep their curious minds busy.

Read the feature

Cats love sunny windows because they provide warmth, natural light, and an interesting view of the outside world.

Watching birds, people, and moving leaves keeps cats entertained and stimulates their natural curiosity.

A sunny windowsill can also become a comfortable place for cats to relax, sleep, and enjoy the warmth.
